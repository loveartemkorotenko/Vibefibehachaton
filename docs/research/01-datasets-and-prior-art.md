## Closed or risky list
- Closed or partially closed: ЄДР address/contact/KVED fields (filtered); debtors register and 15 other Minjust registries (status of most after 2026 not confirmed); construction data partially open, some objects closed until end of martial law (C, https://thepage.ua/ua/news/ukrayina-vidkrila-publichnu-mapu-ta-reyestr-obyektiv-budivnictva).
- Token or key needed: alerts.in.ua, ukrainealarm, Opendatabot, YouControl (paid).
- Fragile: Kyiv GTFS on bare IP http, DTEK/YASNO behind bot protection, 1551 monthly files up to 95 MB.
- Shelters caveat: other cities publish shelters as CSV per municipality on data.gov.ua (e.g. Lviv oblast, 141 hits, updated 2026-11-15), schemas differ.

## Top 3 non-obvious combinations (all fully verified open today)
1. NHSU contracts (stroke/MI/maternity package per facility) + Доступні ліки points + Kyiv shelters/GTFS: "Emergency-ready health map": for a given address, nearest hospital with acute stroke/MI package, whether the route has an open shelter, and where reimbursed medicine is dispensed.
2. NHSU declarations per clinic + 1551 appeals + SaveEcoBot: neighbourhood health-and-living pressure score (clinics near capacity, utility complaints, air quality) for people choosing where to rent/move.
3. Kyiv air-alert history + shelters (invalid/working_time fields) + пункти незламності: "accessible-shelter coverage gaps": districts where shelters lack accessibility or open hours versus real alert duration, for city/NGO advocacy.
