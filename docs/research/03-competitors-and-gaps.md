IMPORTANT limits of this pass:
- Most product facts come from search summaries of media/store pages (grade C). Only 2 pages were fetched in full (marked A/B below). opendatabot.ua/sources returned HTTP 403.
- "Gap" statements below mean "I did not find this in the sources I read", not "the product lacks it". Everything not checked on the product's own site is UNVERIFIED. Verify the 2-3 you pick before pitching.
- No "Світлобот" found in search (UNVERIFIED that it exists).
- Not covered (no time): Prozorro Market citizen angle, Privat/monobank open data, "Карта лікарень" ratings, SaveEcoBot depth, LUN stats (only LUN Місто shelters/accessibility/air seen).
